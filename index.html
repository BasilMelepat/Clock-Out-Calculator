⚠️ Reset all data?

This permanently deletes everything Clock me Out has saved in this browser:

  • Your Daily Log, every month
  • Clock-in time, target hours, breaks, and your running-late and departure plans
  • Holidays and absences
  • Your name

Your light/dark choice is kept. This can't be undone, so use Copy Log in the Daily Log first if you want a backup.

🗓️ Holidays & absences
Holidays and absent days aren't counted as working days left in the Month Goal. Both are also picked up from your attendance sheet when you import it.
